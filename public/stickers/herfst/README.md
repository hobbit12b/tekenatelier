# Herfst in het heksenbos

`stickervel.png` bevat twaalf transparante illustraties (1448 × 1086 pixels), gemaakt met de ingebouwde ImageGen-tool. De genormaliseerde uitsneden staan in `../manifest.json`. De app maakt daarvan bij eerste gebruik zelfstandige PNG-stickers. Dit is een gegenereerde startcollectie; de resolutie per sticker is circa 350 pixels en geen vervanging voor hoogwaardige drukwerkmasters van 1024 pixels.

## Gebruikte prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Design ONE transparent sticker sprite sheet for a preschool drawing app, landscape aspect ratio 4:3. EXACTLY 4 equal columns and 3 equal rows: twelve isolated illustrations. All icons must fit within the middle 70% of their equal grid cell. Keep wide empty transparent gutters (15% on every side of every cell), no overlap, no background scene, no sheet border, no white sticker outlines. Each image must be completely separable by slicing the sheet into 4x3 equal rectangles.
Style: beautiful friendly children's picture-book wax crayon and colored pencil illustrations, visible tactile pigment and hand drawn edges. Warm autumn palette: pumpkin orange, muted purple, golden yellow, teal, moss green, cocoa brown. Rich detail but strong simple shapes readable as small stickers. Soft rounded friendly faces. No photorealism or vector appearance. No words, letters, captions, or numbers. True transparent background.
Strict subject placement left to right:
Top row: a smiling little witch with round face, purple pointed hat and orange dress holding a tiny wand, full body; a crooked purple witch hat with orange ribbon; a straw broom with a warm wooden handle on a diagonal; a teal bubbling magic cauldron with a few golden bubbles.
Middle row: a cheerful red fly agaric mushroom with cream spots and rounded beige stem; a cute smiling hedgehog with russet quills; a plump friendly brown owl with big amber eyes on a short branch; a fluffy orange squirrel sitting holding a small nut.
Bottom row: one golden orange maple autumn leaf; one acorn with brown textured cap and greenish gold nut; a friendly golden crescent moon with sleepy face; one large warm golden magical five-point star with two tiny surrounding sparkles.
IMPORTANT: Every complete subject centered exactly at its grid cell center, 70% maximum cell width and height; keep 15% transparent border within EACH cell. Transparent gaps between all twelve images. All twelve equally polished with consistent hand-drawn crayon technique.
